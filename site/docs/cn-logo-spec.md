# Corenation Logo Exploration

**Brand:** Corenation Active  
**Status:** Two families in parallel — no final master selected

## Design goal

Two families are on the table:

1. **New systems** — six marks that leave the badge language behind:
   **CN abstraction** (letters share strokes, folds, or negative space) and
   **core anatomy** (obliques, the linea alba, and diagonal fascial tension become the symbol).
2. **Hexagon heritage** — five variations built directly on the original closed-hexagon badge
   (`reference-original-hexagon.jpg`), keeping the closed silhouette and the angular N.

## Concepts

| # | Direction | System | Design signal |
|---|-----------|--------|---------------|
| 01 | **Continuum** | CN abstraction | A fluid C-to-N continuous line |
| 02 | **Fold** | CN abstraction | Two angular planes sharing edges |
| 03 | **Counterform** | CN abstraction | C and N cut into the negative space |
| 04 | **Linea** | Core anatomy | Abdominal walls and the central linea alba |
| 05 | **Oblique** | Core anatomy | Six modular rib-to-waist compression bands |
| 06 | **Crossbrace** | Core anatomy / N hybrid | Curved core walls crossed by a fascial sling |

## Hexagon heritage variations

| # | Variation | Treatment | Best use |
|---|-----------|-----------|----------|
| H1 | **Heritage** | Double outline, point-up, beveled N | Faithful modernization of the badge |
| H2 | **Emboss** | Solid badge, negative-space N | Tonal black-on-black, rubber patches |
| H3 | **Inline** | Hairline twin outlines, slim N | Foil stamps, embossing, zipper pulls |
| H4 | **Facet** | Chamfered corners | Equipment and hardware energy |
| H5 | **Sideline** | Rotated to side points | Wide chest prints and waistbands |

## Production notes

- Keep every mark monochrome during selection; color should not rescue weak geometry.
- Test at 42px, 30px, and 20px before choosing a direction.
- Convert the Continuum stroke to an outlined path before sending final artwork to embroidery or molding.
- Preserve the center gaps in Linea and Oblique; thread spread can close them below 12mm.
- Inline (H3) is for flat processes only; its hairlines will not survive embroidery below 40mm.
- Emboss (H2) is the safest hexagon variation for rubber patches and tonal black-on-black.
- Run trademark and similarity screening only after narrowing to one or two directions.
