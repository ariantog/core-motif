## What changed in v2.0 (read this first)

v1.0 was a strong **men's kanji** ops list. It did not give the company a brand direction, or checklists the other teams can run. Revisions:

### Visual Rules
- [ ] Base colors: olive `#4A5240`, black, charcoal grey — max 3 structural colors per piece.
- [ ] Accent colors: cream `#D6CFB5`, gold `#D4AF37`, red `#C41E3A` — **one hero accent** per product face.
- [ ] Brand lead selected one direction from [`readable-wordmark.md`](readable-wordmark.md) before a new PO. Open Set is recommended. No hex, no coin.
- [ ] Kanji on apparel uses **black rectangular patch** (shorts/tees) or **gold/cream on dark overlay** (belts).
- [ ] Kanji stroke style: bold brush on belts; clean bold strokes on small patches (legibility at 3m).
- [ ] Wordmark: `CORENATION` or `CORE NATION` — all caps, wide sans-serif only.
- [ ] No more than **one hero kanji** per visible product panel.
- [ ] Mesh panels stay **kanji-free** (charcoal strip + black mesh at hem only).

## 1. Brand direction — preferable, not premium

**Positioning in one sentence:**  
Corenation is the training brand you actually wear to train — same class of fabric as Nike / Adidas / Lululemon, priced so you buy it twice, styled like Adidas (locker room, not marble spa).

**We are:** performance, disciplined, local, useful, athletic, community.  
**We are not:** luxury, exclusive, yoga-boutique, influencer-spa, "elevated lifestyle."

### What "Adidas energy" means here (use this when briefing photographers / designers)

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Gym floor, rack, track, padel court, sweat optional | Marble, champagne, silk robes, gold dust overlays |
| One strong graphic or one flower cluster | Logo wallpaper, all-over luxury monogram |
| Athletes who look like they train | Posed luxury models who never lift |
| Olive / black / cream for men; pink / lilac for women | Mixing men's olive with women's pink in one SKU or one feed grid |
| Price that feels *smart* | Deepest Shopee discount bin **or** boutique markup |
| "Built to move" / "train in it" | "Limited atelier drop" / "exclusive luxury" |

### Words — use / never use

| Never use | Use instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| premium, luxury, exclusive, elevated, atelier, couture, opulent | performance, training, quality fabric, built to move, everyday athlete |
| limited luxury drop | this season's training drop |
| invest in yourself | gear that lasts the block |
| ultra-soft spa | moisture-wick, 4-way stretch, wash-tested |

**Indonesian + English mix is OK.** First mention of any kanji always includes romanization + meaning (*力 — chikara, power*). Flowers: Indonesian common name + English (*sakura / bunga sakura*, *lavender / lavendel*).

### Shared brand marks (every line)

- [ ] Wordmark is `CORENATION` or `CORE NATION` — all caps, wide sans-serif only. Letter **A** may use the diagonal-cut custom A from the belt samples — do not invent a third A.
- [ ] Logo mark on **new** work is the readable `CORENATION` wordmark ([`readable-wordmark.md`](readable-wordmark.md)). Do not reprint the hex or coin. White/cream on dark; charcoal on light pink/lilac.
- [ ] Max **one hero motif** per visible panel (one kanji, or one flower cluster, or one culture graphic).
- [ ] No competing line motifs on the same SKU (no sakura + 龍, no lavender + kanji patch).
- [ ] Hangtag / care label: Corenation, composition, care, **line code** (MEN / WMN / HJB / RUN / PDL). Wrong line code = reject.

### Palette lock (do not invent seasonal "premium" golds)

**Men / Culture Run (dark):** olive `#4A5240` · black `#000000` · charcoal `#3A3A3A` · cream `#D6CFB5` · gold `#D4AF37` · red `#C41E3A`

**Women sakura (pink SKUs):** sakura pink `#E8A4B4` · deep rose `#C45B78` · cream `#F6EDE8` · charcoal (structure only) · blossom white

**Women lavender (lilac SKUs):** lilac `#C5B4D8` · lavender `#8B6FA8` · dusk purple `#5C4A72` · cream `#F3EEF6` · charcoal (structure only)

**Women Culture Run (pastel):** powder `#B8D4E8` · seafoam `#9ED4C4` · apricot `#E8A888` · mint `#C8E4D4` · sakura `#E8A4B4` · lilac `#C5B4D8` · charcoal (marks only)

**Hijab:** either floral palette **or** neutrals (black, cream, olive, navy). No gold-foil luxury script.

## 3. Channel playbooks

### A. Shopee (majority = gym accs)

Owner: Marketplace

This is where gloves, belts, straps, knee supports, Hyrox bags, totes **pay the rent**. Apparel can live here; accs get the hero slots.

- [ ] Accs have the **first three gallery slots** (main + detail + in-use). Apparel is image 4+ unless the SKU *is* apparel.
- [ ] Title format by line (see line sections). Always start with `Corenation`.
- [ ] Main image: white / light grey, motif readable as a thumbnail.
- [ ] Price: preferable tier — same quality story, **not** cheapest listing, **not** 3× boutique. Review vs Nike/Adidas outlet **and** vs local copycats before going live.
- [ ] Description block 1 = fabric + use (not "premium luxury").
- [ ] Description block 2 = motif meaning (kanji / flower / culture graphic).
- [ ] Description block 3 = fit, care, size.
- [ ] Cross-listing: women's floral template ≠ men's kanji template. No color-swap of the same stock photo across lines.

### B. Offline dept stores (majority = apparel)

Owner: Offline VM

Apparel (men / women / hijab, roughly equal) is what walks out of Central, Muku, and Cilandak.

- [ ] Men's bay, women's floral bay, hijab bay are **separate**. Opposite wall or a fixture break is enough — do not rainbow-mix one rack.
- [ ] Mannequins wear a full look (tee + short / set + hijab). Accs are add-on (belt on mannequin, gloves on peg).
- [ ] Zone signage uses the seasonal **one hero motif** (e.g. 龍, sakura branch, 走 wave).
- [ ] Price cards match Shopee titles (same product name). QR to Shopee optional, never a different SKU name.
- [ ] Weekly Friday photo audit of all three stores sent to brand lead (same angles: bay, mannequin, table).

### C. HQ warehouse / padel clubs (B2B)

Owner: HQ / padel

Clubs pick fabrics; we cut and sew; the brand on the chest is still Corenation.

- [ ] Club signs a one-pager: hex C + CORENATION stay; club name may appear as a **secondary** mark (sleeve or hem), never replacing the hex C.
- [ ] Colors chosen from the **approved palette** (or a written exception from brand lead). No raw neon unless approved.
- [ ] No kanji / floral / culture graphic unless the club ordered that line — default club kit is clean performance.
- [ ] Tech pack still has wash-test, size run, and Corenation care label.
- [ ] Photo the finished kit on court (padel) for @corenationactive — community, not luxury lookbook.

## 4. Pre-production (all lines)

Owner: Design / production · Sign-off: Brand lead

### Design file

- [ ] Motif verified: kanji = native speaker or trusted Japanese source (stroke count, **Japanese form**, no vulgar compound). Floral = botanically readable (sakura ≠ generic five-petal; lavender = spikes, not sakura). Culture graphic = one story, not a tourist collage.
- [ ] Romanization / flower name / graphic name written on the tech pack.
- [ ] Colors matched to the palette table (CMYK + nearest Pantone noted).
- [ ] Placement matches the line grid (see spec docs).
- [ ] Min sizes: shorts patch ≥ 60×80mm; belt hero 40–60mm tall; running back graphic readable at 3m; floral cluster readable at 1.5m.
- [ ] Print / embroidery / subli method named. **Wash-test 50+ cycles** required before PO.
- [ ] Size run: S–XXL (and hijab size range) — motif scales, does not crowd a size S or float on XXL.
- [ ] Interior labels: composition, care, Corenation, **correct line code**.

### Sample

- [ ] Sample photographed on neutral ground (men: olive + black visible; women: pink or lilac visible; run: graphic visible).
- [ ] Legibility signed off (kanji 3m / floral 1.5m / run graphic 3m) with a gym-floor or store-aisle photo.
- [ ] Color vs swatch in daylight **and** indoor gym / store light.
- [ ] Stitch / patch / print adhesion pull test documented.
- [ ] Design lead **and** brand lead both signed the sample form.

## 8. Culture Run — running shirts & tanks

Owner: Brand lead + Design · Social: Active and/or Men (see drop)

**What this line is:** lightweight running tees, tanks, singlets with **one cultural graphic** — not a lifting-belt patch, not a floral all-over.

**What "culture" means here:** the stories this brand already trains with — Japanese graphic language (wave, summit, blossom path, 走) plus one **Home Soil** graphic (Indonesian mountain / dawn run). It is **not** a tourist souvenir print and **not** a batik costume.

### Voice & visual

- [ ] Copy is pace, session, community run. Use: *run the city, keep the line, dawn club.* Not *conquer nations*, not luxury marathon.
- [ ] Fabric looks **light** in photos (mesh, singlet, split hem). If it photographs like a lifting tee, recut or relight.
- [ ] Graphic is one idea: Wave (走), Summit, Bloom Path (women), Dusk Lilac (women), Home Soil, or one pastel story (Mist Wave / Apricot Brush / Mint Marble / Blush / Lilac).
- [ ] **No embroidery patches** on running SKUs. Hex C + 走 + waves are **print / dye-sub** only (raised heat-transfer is OK if it looks printed, not a sewn badge).
- [ ] **Gradation required** on v2 pieces (Nedao Vortex / Aurora / Blue Hour energy). Do not ship a solid charcoal tee with a sticker graphic.
- [ ] Placement: all-over color; 走 or the culture graphic lives in the dye on the back. Front stays quiet (printed hex C + wordmark).
- [ ] Men Culture Run may use 走. Women Culture Run may use blossom/lavender **as printed color**, still no kanji patch.
- [ ] Olive tactical stays on the lifting line.

### Shot list

- [ ] Ghost mannequin or runner in motion (track, park, city dawn).
- [ ] Back graphic is the hero frame.
- [ ] Front + side so the running cut is obvious (armhole, hem).
- [ ] Pair with shorts that are **not** the kanji patch short (avoid mixed-line confusion).

### Shopee title

`Corenation Culture Run [Tee|Tank|Singlet] — [Wave|Summit|Bloom Path|Dusk|Home Soil|Mist Wave|Apricot Brush|Mint Marble|Blush Watercolor|Lilac Watercolor]`

### Store VM

- [ ] Endcap or hanging "run wall" — shirts face out so the **back graphic** is visible (use a two-way hang or a back-facing mannequin).
- [ ] Do not hang Culture Run inside the lifting-belt table.

## 8b. Padel court kit

Owner: Brand lead + Design · Social: Active and/or Men

**What this line is:** consumer padel polo, tee, tank, shorts, and women skirt. No kanji. No hex C. No floral. v4 is sewn panels + contrast stitch. Women stay pastel. Men v4 is earth.

**What it is not:** HQ club-custom kits (those still keep hex C — see §3C). Not Culture Run. Not sakura / lavender.

### Voice & visual

- [ ] Copy is court, pair, session. Use: *play the glass, stay light, club night.* Not warrior kanji, not luxury clubhouse.
- [ ] **No marks on the garment face.** Factory may keep Corenation on the care label only.
- [ ] Women v4: **Petal Seam** (princess panels + cream flatlock) and **Citrus Stitch** (raglan / yoke + apricot overlock). Skirt is women-only.
- [ ] Men v4: **Clay Court** (angular panels + sandstone topstitch) and **Moss Baseline** (raglan / side panels + sand flatlock). Earth tones only — not mint / periwinkle.
- [ ] Men v3: **Crosscourt Mesh** (diagonal + net dissolve) and **Orbit Serve** (off-center halo + pinlines).
- [ ] Women v3: **Rally Ribbon** (S-curve + micro dashes) and **Halo Serve** (side-origin arcs + transition ticks). Skirt is women-only.
- [ ] Transition pattern is tonal, follows one color boundary, and covers no more than 12% of the garment face.
- [ ] Men v2: **Reef Trio** (3 color · seafoam → sky → periwinkle) and **Dual Glass** (2 color · teal → periwinkle).
- [ ] Women v2: **Clay Bloom** (3 color · peach → rose → lilac) and **Dual Sherbet** (2 color · apricot → pistachio). Skirt is women-only.
- [ ] v1 one-hue washes (Glass Court, Harbor Haze, Sunrise Clay, Lemon Sherbet) stay in the archive bay. New women samples must show 2 or 3 distinct pastels. New men v4 samples must show 2 or 3 distinct earth hues.
- [ ] Do not put a men's fade on a women's cut or the reverse.
- [ ] Binding / collar / waistband stay inside the same color family. Contrast stitch is tonal. No black logo patch.

### Shopee title

`Corenation Padel [Polo|Tee|Tank|Shorts|Skirt] — [Petal Seam|Citrus Stitch|Clay Court|Moss Baseline]`

### Store VM

- [ ] Own padel fixture or endcap. Men earth stitch left, women pastel stitch + skirts right.
- [ ] Do not hang next to 龍 shorts or sakura belts without a break.
