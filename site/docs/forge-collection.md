**Brand:** Corenation Active  
**Line:** Forge - construction-led gym tees, tanks, and training shorts  
**Accounts:** @corenationactive / @corenationmen  
**Positioning:** Warehouse-floor kit. Heavier than Pace. No kanji patch. No floral. No race mesh. Nike training / Adidas Designed for Training / Lululemon License to Train energy, made on normal sewing machines.

## Art direction (locked)

No printed graphics. Design comes from **cut, seam, webbing, bartacks, welt pockets, and heavier fabric**. Factory may keep Corenation on the care label only.

| Rule | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| Marks | Blank chest, back, collar, waistband, hem | Kanji, hex C, wordmark, flowers, waves, dye-sub |
| Design | Interlock panels, webbing channels, welt / bellows pockets, visible bartacks | Running mesh maps, laser holes, bonded edges, cargo MOLLE walls |
| Fabric | 165-185 gsm interlock or pique tops; 130-150 gsm stretch-woven shorts | 120 gsm race jersey, cotton fleece hoodie weight, shiny tricot |
| Cut | Gym block: regular/boxy men, fitted hip-length women, 8 inch men short, 5 inch women short | Race drop-tail, 3-5 inch run split, polo, skirt, crop |
| Gender | Separate stories and cuts | One panel map shared across men and women |
| Pairing | Matching short in the same story | Kanji patch short, Pace run short, floral set |

This is the **gym-floor wall**. Pace stays the run wall. Men kanji stays the patch / belt wall.

### Factory capability lock

Every piece must be producible with:

- straight-stitch / lockstitch;
- 4-thread overlock;
- 2-needle coverstitch;
- webbing and eyelet setting;
- #5 coil-zip installation; and
- bartack, or a short narrow zigzag if no bartack machine is available.

**No laser cutter, perforation service, seam-bonding machine, glue, or heat-sealed edge.**

## Garment block

- **Tee:** 165-185 gsm interlock. Men regular with a slightly boxy shoulder. Women fitted, hip length, **not cropped**. 20-25 mm side split. Thick rib collar. No polo. No race drop-tail.
- **Tank:** men = drop-arm gym tank, straps 50-60 mm. Women = scoop training tank, straps 35-40 mm. Fold-over or webbing bind. Not a race singlet.
- **Men short:** 8 inch inseam training short, 50 mm encased elastic, internal drawcord, one #5 zip pocket, optional brief liner.
- **Women short:** 5 inch inseam high-rise training short, 35 mm encased elastic, one #5 zip pocket, brief liner.

Binding, waistband, and stitch stay inside the story color family.

### Construction rules

- One construction idea per story.
- Panels wrap front to back. Do not reset the idea at a side seam.
- Webbing is woven nylon tape sewn as a channel, bind, or bartacked tab. Not printed stripes.
- Pockets are real welt or bellows bags. Pocket sits at hip / thigh, never on the bust.
- Bartacks are 8-10 mm at splits, webbing ends, welt corners, and zipper ends.
- Olive may appear on Rack Web. Do not add kanji, hex, or floral to that olive.

| Code | Material | Target | Use |
|------|----------|--------|-----|
| J2 | Polyester-spandex interlock or pique | 165-185 gsm | Tee and tank body |
| W2 | Mechanical-stretch woven | 130-150 gsm | Shorts outer shell |
| L1 | Lightweight tricot | 75-90 gsm | Shorts brief liner |
| E2 | Knitted elastic | 35-50 mm | Shorts waistband |
| Z3 | Nylon coil zipper | #5, 14-16 cm | Phone / rear security pocket |

| EY | Matte gunmetal eyelet | 6-8 mm ID | Drawcord and webbing ends |
| P1 | Filled piping cord | 2 mm finished | Optional panel seams |

Approve stretch recovery, shrinkage, colorfastness, and pocket bounce before bulk. A phone in the welt pocket must stay put through a squat and a bench set.

## Stitch and seam standard

| Operation | Standard construction | Allowance / setting |
|-----------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| Main assembly | 4-thread overlock | 8 mm seam allowance (heavier fabric) |
| Visible seam | Overlock inward + 2-needle coverstitch | 6 mm; 5-6 mm needle gauge |
| Webbing | Twin-needle or lockstitch box + bartacks | Catch both ends into a seam or bartack |
| Eyelet | Pressed gunmetal, washers on the inside | 6-8 mm inside diameter |
| Tee/tank hem | 2-needle coverstitch | 20 mm turnback |
| Binding | Fold-over elastic, self-fabric, or webbing | 8-12 mm finished |
| Welt pocket | Lockstitch welt, bag, corner bartacks | 80-90 x 150-160 mm bag |
| Bellows pocket | 15-20 mm gusset, #5 zip, corner bartacks | Right thigh on shorts |
| Stress point | Bartack or narrow zigzag | 8-10 mm |

No exposed overlock at underarm, neck, or inner thigh. Run a squat / bench / carry wear test before approving bulk.

## Story construction details

### Men

#### Cinder Block

- Rust panel begins underarm and wraps to the opposite rear hem. Concrete wedge supports the side seam.
- J2 charcoal body. Visible rust coverstitch and rust bartacks.
- Tank uses 50-60 mm drop-arm straps. Short is 8 inch, not a 5 inch run split.

#### Rack Web

- 25 mm cream webbing channel across the back yoke, caught into both shoulder seams.
- Two gunmetal eyelets and bartacked webbing tails. Front hem gets one webbing tab only.
- Olive is a saddle / side panel, not an all-over tactical print.
- Shorts use webbing belt-loop channels at both hips. No MOLLE wall.

#### Plate Pocket

- Slate yoke on tee and tank. Right-side welt phone pocket below the ribcage.
- Shorts use a 20 mm bellows zip pocket on the right thigh plus a rear security zip.
- Pocket bag must clear a squat without hitting the hip crease.

### Women

#### Kiln Seam

- Clay J2 throughout. Concrete princess and spine seams with pewter coverstitch.
- Shape comes from seams, not a floral or watercolor.
- 5 inch high-rise short. Hip length tee and tank, not cropped.

#### Anvil Strap

- 20 mm rust webbing binds neck and armholes / sleeve openings.
- A webbing bridge or hip channel is bartacked at both ends.
- Charcoal body. Capable gym look, not jewelry hardware.

#### Slab Pocket

- Rust side panels wrap front to back. Right-hip welt pocket on tee and tank.
- Shorts add a 15 mm bellows zip on the right front thigh plus a rear zip.
- Pocket sits at the hip, never on the bust.
