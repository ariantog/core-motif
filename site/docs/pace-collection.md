## Art direction (locked)

No printed graphics. Design comes from **cut, seam, mesh, bonded edge, and laser-cut ventilation**. Factory may keep Corenation on the care label only.

| Rule | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| Marks | Blank chest, back, collar, waistband, hem | Kanji, hex C, CORENATION, Nike-style swoosh, 3-stripe logo, flowers, waves |
| Design | Sewn panels, mapped seams, mesh yokes, laser-cut heat zones, bonded hems | Dye-sub print, marble, watercolor, all-over graphic, fake printed seams |
| Fabric | Lightweight jersey + air-mesh + pin-dot knit. Matte, 4-way stretch | Heavy cotton, shiny tricot, lifting-tee weight, olive tactical ripstop |
| Hue count | 2 or 3 readable hues, one construction idea | White shirt with a sticker, one solid with no structure |
| Gender | Separate stories and cuts | One panel map shared across men and women SKUs |
| Pairing | Matching short in the same story | Kanji patch short, padel skirt, floral set |

This is the **no-print run wall**. Culture Run stays the graphic wall.

## Garment rules

- **Tee:** short-sleeve running jersey. Men regular with a slight drop-tail. Women fitted, hip length, **not cropped**. Split hem. Crew or bound scoop. No polo collar.
- **Tank:** men = deep-armhole race tank (wide strap or racer singlet). Women = racerback scoop. Bound edges, not raw-cut unless the story is bonded.
- **Shorts:** men 5–7" split running short with brief liner + one zip pocket. Women 3–4" fitted running short with liner. Tonal waistband — no logo patch. Not a padel 7" court short. Not a lifting short.

Binding, waistband, and stitch stay **inside the same color family**. Contrast stitch may be one step lighter or darker. No black structure panels on the women's stories except Curve Pace waistband/binding.

### Construction rules

- One construction per story. Do not mix laser-cut + color-block + mesh cape on one SKU.
- Panels and mesh wrap front to back. Do not reset the idea at a side seam.
- Laser-cut holes go **through** the fabric (Vent Map). They are not a printed dot pattern.
- Seams are real: flatlock, coverstitch, or bonded. Do not draw fake stitch in the middle of a panel.
- Reflective is a 2mm pinline at one seam max (Cloud Yoke). Not a graphic hit.

### Color notes

- **Vent Map:** ink `#1C2228` + slate `#3D454C` + mist `#C5CDD1`. Dark technical. On / Adizero.
- **Stride Panel:** black `#0E1012` + slate `#3D454C` + rust `#C45C38`. Nike block energy — rust is the hero, not red `#C41E3A`.
- **Cloud Yoke:** bone `#E6E2D8` + cool gray `#9AA3A8` + ink `#2A3138`. Light On Cloud kit. Not padel aqua.
- **Swift Map:** sand `#E6D4C4` + blush `#D8B8B4` + fog `#C8C2BC`. Lululemon Swiftly — seams are the design. Not sakura floral.
- **Aero Racer:** rose clay `#D4A090` + pearl `#F2EBE4` + slate `#6A6460`. Mesh spine is the hero. Not Culture Run bloom marble.
- **Curve Pace:** bone `#F0E8DC` + apricot `#E4B090` + charcoal `#3A3A3A` (binding only). Adidas women curve. Apricot is not pink lock.
