# Corenation Pace - Standard-Sew Running Kit

**Brand:** Corenation Active  
**Line:** Pace - construction-led running tees, tanks, and shorts  
**Accounts:** @corenationactive / @corenationmen  
**Positioning:** Lululemon / Nike AeroSwift / Adidas Adizero / On Cloud energy, executed with normal apparel-factory machines. The garment is the graphic. Not Culture Run. Not padel. Not a lifting tee.

---

## Art direction (locked)

No printed graphics. Design comes from **cut, seam, purchased mesh, piping, pleats, overlap vents, rib panels, and visible stitch**. Factory may keep Corenation on the care label only.

| Rule | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| Marks | Blank chest, back, collar, waistband, hem | Kanji, hex C, CORENATION, swoosh-like marks, 3-stripe marks, flowers, waves |
| Design | Sewn panels, mapped seams, bought mesh, piping, pleats, coverstitch | Dye-sub print, marble, watercolor, laser cutting, bonded/glued edges, fake printed seams |
| Fabric | Lightweight jersey + supplier mesh + stretch woven. Matte, quick-dry | Heavy cotton, shiny tricot shell, lifting-tee weight, tactical ripstop |
| Hue count | 2 or 3 readable hues, one construction idea | One solid with no structure, or several competing details |
| Gender | Separate stories and cuts | One panel map shared across men and women |
| Pairing | Matching short in the same story | Kanji patch short, padel skirt, floral set |

This is the **no-print run wall**. Culture Run stays the graphic wall.

### Factory capability lock

Every piece must be producible with:

- straight-stitch / lockstitch;
- 4-thread overlock;
- 2-needle coverstitch;
- elastic application;
- standard coil-zip installation; and
- bartack, or a short narrow zigzag if no bartack machine is available.

Use commercially available eyelet, pinhole, or warp-knit mesh yardage. **No laser cutter, perforation service, seam-bonding machine, glue, or heat-sealed edge is required anywhere in this line.**

## Garment block

- **Tee:** short-sleeve run jersey. Men regular with a 15 mm drop-tail. Women fitted, hip length, **not cropped**. 18-20 mm side split. No polo collar.
- **Tank:** men use a deep armhole and wide race straps. Women use a racerback scoop. Finish neck and armholes with fold-over or self-fabric binding.
- **Men short:** 5-6 inch split shell, built-in brief, 35 mm encased elastic, internal drawcord, one rear zip pocket.
- **Women short:** 3-4 inch split or tulip shell, built-in brief, clean high-rise waistband, one rear zip pocket.

Binding, waistband, and stitch stay inside the story's color family. Contrast stitch may be one step lighter or darker.

### Construction rules

- One construction idea per story. Do not mix zip stand + stash pocket + gusset + layer hem on one SKU.
- Panels and mesh wrap front to back. Do not reset the idea at a side seam.
- Ventilation comes from a **separate purchased mesh pattern piece** or an open overlap backed by mesh.
- Seams are real: overlock + coverstitch, lockstitch, or piping.
- Free vent and pleat edges are overlocked, turned once, and single-needle topstitched.
- Reinforce split tops, vent ends, pleat stops, and zipper ends with 8-10 mm bartacks.
- Optional reflective is 2 mm sew-in piping at one seam maximum. Plain piping is the default.

## Stitch and seam standard

| Operation | Standard construction | Allowance / setting |
|-----------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| Main assembly | 4-thread overlock | 6 mm seam allowance |
| Visible seam | Overlock inward + 2-needle coverstitch | 6 mm; 5-6 mm needle gauge |
| Piping seam | Single-needle lockstitch, then overlock | 6 mm; 2 mm finished piping |
| Tee/tank hem | 2-needle coverstitch | 18 mm turnback |
| Binding | Fold-over elastic or self-fabric binder | 8-10 mm finished |
| Vent/pleat edge | Overlock, turn once, single-needle topstitch | 6 mm turn |
| Stress point | Bartack or narrow zigzag substitute | 8-10 mm |
| Shorts pocket | #3 coil zipper, lockstitch + bartacks | 12-14 cm opening |
| Waistband | 35 mm elastic fully encased | Mark and match four quarters |
| Quarter zip | Reverse-coil #3, lockstitch tape, chin guard, folded garage | 15-18 cm opening |
| Stash pocket | Power-mesh panel, 10 mm folded elastic top, bartacked divisions | Bottom caught into hem or waistband |

No exposed overlock should touch the underarm or inner thigh. Run a 10 km wear test before approving bulk.

## Story construction details

### Men

#### Air Grid

- **Tee:** M1 curved side-rib panels and V upper-back yoke; mist coverstitch; J1 drop-tail.
- **Tank:** M1 full side panels and kite back panel; fold-over neck / arm binding.
- **Shorts:** W1 shell, M1 outer-thigh panels and shallow back yoke, L1 liner, Z1 rear pocket.
- Mesh holes are created by the mesh supplier. The factory only cuts the pattern pieces and sews them into the garment.

#### Stride Panel

- Rust panel begins underarm and wraps to the opposite rear hem; slate wedge supports the side seam.
- Join panels with overlock and visible rust coverstitch.
- Use turned, coverstitched hems. Replace any bonded sample edge with this finish.
- Keep rust out of the liner and inner thigh.

#### Cloud Yoke

- M1 shoulder cape is sewn into front-yoke, raglan, and back-yoke seams.
- Use plain mist piping by default. Sew-in reflective piping is optional if locally available.
- Finish all openings with ink binding and all hems with coverstitch.

#### Back Draft

- Mineral cape overlaps M1 underlay by 35 mm on tops and 25 mm on shorts.
- Secure the free edge only at three bartacks so the vent remains open.
- Keep mesh under every opening so skin is not exposed.
- Shorts repeat the construction below the rear zip pocket and inside the side split.

#### Relay Rib

- R1 runs underarm-to-hem; on shorts it stops above the side split.
- Insert P1 only at the front rib edge. Overlock the rear rib edge before coverstitch.
- Rib must recover to at least 95% after a 30-minute stretch test.
- Do not place rib at the crotch or inner thigh.

#### Axis Split

- Teal saddle crosses front and back as one asymmetric pattern story; do not mirror it.
- M2 appears only at the opposite-side gusset.
- Match the diagonal at shoulder and side seams within 3 mm.
- Shorts carry the diagonal from front waist into the opposite rear yoke.

#### Cadence Zip

- **Tee:** 18 cm #3 reverse-coil quarter zip, chin guard, folded garage, storm saddle with ice piping.
- **Tank:** 15 cm zip into a 25 mm stand collar; fold-over armhole binding; wide male straps.
- **Shorts:** 16 cm diagonal hip zip with garage plus 12 cm rear coil pocket; storm saddle + ice piping.
- Zipper tape is lockstitched. Do not leave raw tape against skin. Bartack both zipper ends.

#### Trail Stow

- **Tee / tank:** M3 sand power-mesh lower-back panel, 17-18 cm tall, three compartments, 10 mm folded elastic top.
- Catch pocket sides into side seams and the pocket bottom into the hem. Do not float the pocket on the body.
- **Shorts:** three 9 cm rear-waist gel pockets plus one #3 coil security zip above the center pocket.
- This is a running stash, not a cycling jersey pocket wall and not a hydration vest.

#### Motion Gusset

- **Tee:** two-piece articulated raglan; M2 ember mesh underarm gusset continues down the side rib to hem.
- **Tank:** two-piece steel shoulder saddle; ember mesh side gusset from armhole to hem.
- **Shorts:** ember four-way jersey diamond crotch gusset under the brief liner; steel outer-thigh panels.
- Keep bulky stitch off the inner thigh. Ember coverstitch is for outer seams only.

### Women

#### Swift Map

- Use J1 throughout; shape comes from princess, shoulder, and center-back seams.
- Overlock inward and add fog 2-needle coverstitch on mapped seams.
- Finish openings with self-fabric or fold-over binding; no bonded edges.

#### Aero Racer

- M2 spine runs nape-to-hem; the tank narrows into a racerback.
- Join mesh to J1 with overlock + coverstitch; bind neck and armholes in slate.
- Shorts use M2 only at side splits and rear yoke, always backed by L1 liner.

#### Curve Pace

- Apricot panel is a separate cut piece wrapping hip-to-opposite shoulder.
- Clip the curved seam allowance before overlocking; topstitch from the body side.
- Keep charcoal to binding and drawcord only.

#### Crossflow

- Mauve flaps overlap over M1 underlay; skin stays covered through full shoulder movement.
- Turn and topstitch flap edges, then bartack only the ends.
- Tank straps cross over the mesh back; do not stitch the overlap closed.
- Shorts repeat the overlap at side splits and use L1 underneath.

#### Rib Trace

- R1 contour panel follows waist and rear shoulder; P1 sits only on its front edge.
- Do not place rib in crotch or inner thigh.
- Ease rib to J1 / W1 without gathering; maximum seam waviness is 3 mm over 30 cm.

#### Tempo Pleat

- Insert M2 gusset inside an inverted box pleat; the pleat lies closed at rest.
- Bartack the top and stop point, never across the airflow opening.
- Shorts side kick pleat opens 30 mm; L1 liner stays independent.

#### Halo Vent

- Mauve yoke ends in a crescent free edge overlapping M2 pearl mesh by 30 mm on tops and 22 mm on shorts.
- Turn and topstitch the free edge. Three bartacks hold the arc; do not stitch the vent closed.
- Mesh must cover skin through full shoulder movement. Shorts keep L1 liner under the rear vent.

#### Loop Back

- Cut a teardrop or oval opening, then sew M2 shell mesh into the hole so no skin is exposed.
- Finish the opening with rose-taupe fold-over binding. Add a 16-20 mm fabric bridge and bartack both ends.
- Shorts repeat the oval on each outer hip and keep L1 liner behind the mesh.

#### Layer Hem

- Outer J1 / W1 hem curves up 25 mm at each side. M2 cream underlayer is a separate pattern piece.
- Join both layers only at the side seam. Hem each layer on its own machine pass.
- Underlayer extends 12-18 mm below the outer curve at the sides. Bartack only the split tops.
- No glue, fusing, or bonded hem. Shorts keep L1 liner independent of the mesh underlayer.

## Sample approval

- [ ] Correct fabric codes and gsm recorded.
- [ ] Mesh is purchased yardage, not holes cut into body fabric.
- [ ] No glue, bonded edge, or heat-sealed seam.
- [ ] Side split / vent / pleat opens cleanly and returns flat.
- [ ] All stress points reinforced.
- [ ] No exposed seam rub at underarm, neck, crotch, or inner thigh.
- [ ] Rear pocket fits a phone without bounce.
- [ ] 10 km wear test passed.
- [ ] Three wash cycles passed with no seam wave, mesh snag, or color bleed.
- [ ] Front, back, inside, and close-up photos attached to sample record.
