## Brand Design Principles (Derived from Samples)

### Color Palette

| Role | Hex (approx.) | Usage |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Olive base** | `#4A5240` | Primary garment/belt fabric — tactical, grounded, versatile |
| **Black** | `#000000` | Waistbands, patch backgrounds, hardware zones, mesh panels |
| **Charcoal grey** | `#3A3A3A` | Side panels, yoke seams, secondary fabric blocks |
| **Cream** | `#D6CFB5` | Logo, wordmark, secondary kanji accents on belts/accessories |
| **Gold** | `#D4AF37` | Primary kanji on belts, premium accent on accessories |
| **Red accent** | `#C41E3A` (sample-matched) | High-contrast kanji on black rectangular patches (shorts/apparel) |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Hexagon C logo on olive/black, small placement marks |

**Rule:** One hero accent color per product surface. Olive + black structure the piece; kanji gets gold (belts/accessories) or red (apparel patches).

### Typography & Logo

- **Wordmark:** `CORENATION` or `CORE NATION` — wide, bold, all-caps sans-serif. Belts: cream on dark overlay. Shorts waistband: tonal embossed black-on-black.
- **Logo mark:** White hexagon enclosing stylized **C** — use on waistband center patch, lower leg hem, belt near buckle.
- **Japanese vertical text:** Small cream/red vertical kana or kanji beside logo — decorative, not primary messaging. Keep legible at 8–12mm print height.

### Kanji Application Rules

| Method | Best for | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Black rectangular patch** | Shorts lower leg, tee chest/sleeve | Red or gold kanji inside patch; military/tactical hook-and-loop aesthetic; min patch 60×80mm |
| **Direct print / heat transfer** | Tees, tank backs, bag panels | Single-color kanji; use gold or cream on olive, red on black |
| **Embroidered patch** | Belts, gloves, straps | Gold thread on black patch, or cream on olive — premium feel without luxury pricing |
| **Woven label** | Waistband interior, bag lining | Small single kanji as collection code |

### Placement Rules

1. **One hero kanji per visible panel** — avoid competing characters on the same face.
2. **Asymmetric balance:** Logo on one side, kanji patch on opposite leg/hem (see dragon shorts sample).
3. **Belt layout (left → right):** Hardware → hexagon C → vertical JP text → `CORENATION` wordmark → hero kanji on dark curved overlay.
4. **Scale:** Kanji should read at 3m for gym floor visibility; belt kanji ~40–60mm tall; shorts patch kanji ~50–70mm.
5. **Mesh & paneling:** Charcoal side strips + black mesh at hem — do not place kanji over mesh (legibility loss).

### Production Notes for Design Team

- Patch edges: 2mm radius corners or square — match existing dragon shorts spec.
- Color fastness: gold/red kanji must survive 50+ wash cycles (subli or high-grade heat transfer).
- Kanji stroke weight: prefer bold/traditional brush style on belts; clean bold sans-style strokes on apparel patches for small-size clarity.
- **Do not** mix men's kanji with women's floral motifs on the same SKU.
