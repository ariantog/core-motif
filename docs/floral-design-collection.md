## Motif drawing rules

### Sakura

- Five-petal cherry blossom. Petals slightly notched at the tip. Small stamen dots in deep rose.
- Branch: thin, dark, a few blossoms + 1–2 buds. Not a full tree, not a wedding arch.
- Placement: belt — small cluster near the wordmark; gloves — cuff or backhand; apparel — left chest or hem, **or** one sleeve branch.
- Scale: cluster 40–80mm. Individual blossom 12–22mm.

### Lavender

- **Spikes**, not round heads. 3–5 stems, staggered height, small florets.
- Placement: belt — vertical or low horizontal spray; gloves — cuff; apparel — side seam or back yoke.
- Scale: spray 60–120mm tall. Do not cover the full torso.

### What this is not

- All-over liberty print (too boutique).
- Gold foil + script "Paris" (too Alo / too luxury).
- Cartoon kawaii faces on flowers.
- Mixed bouquet (sakura + lavender + peony on one SKU).

**Later (not this drop):** jasmine / *melati* for a hijab-first floral. Do not add it until sakura and lavender are selling cleanly.
