# Corenation CN Logo — Hexagonal C + N

**Brand:** Corenation Active  
**Mark:** Combined **C** (outer frame) + **N** (inner letterform)

## Concept

The original mark used a **closed hexagon** with a stylized **N** inside. The updated mark keeps the same angular N but **opens the hexagon on the right** so the outer frame reads clearly as a stylized **C**.

| Element | Role |
|--------|------|
| Outer frame | Hexagonal **C** — flat top/bottom, pointed left, open gap on the right |
| Inner strokes | Unchanged **N** — three angular strokes, same weight ratio as before |

Together the mark spells **CN** for **CORENATION** without needing the full wordmark at small sizes (belt buckle, hem patch, favicon).

## Color usage

- **Primary on dark:** Cream or white stroke on black, charcoal, or olive `#4A5240`
- **Emboss / tonal:** Black stroke on black fabric (shorts waistband)
- **Minimum size:** 12mm print height for belt patches; 8mm only if stroke weight is increased for production

## Production notes

- Export as vector (SVG/PDF) for embroidery and heat transfer
- Keep stroke caps **square** and joins **miter** to match the angular gym aesthetic
- Do not round the C opening — the gap on the right is intentional and must stay visible at small scale
