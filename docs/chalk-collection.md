## Art direction (locked)

These are **plastisol stamps**, not all-over prints and not embroidery.

| Rule | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| Inks | Cream `#D6CFB5` + rust `#C45C38` | Gold foil, neon, process CMYK, discharge-only art |
| Ground | Charcoal `#1A1C1E` or olive `#4A5240` gym tee | Print on Pace mesh, floral, or a kanji face |
| Art | Flat cartoon animals, stacked Inter-bold quotes | Photoreal fur, gradients, script, movie quotes |
| Count | One stamp per tee | Animal plus quote on the same face |
| Size | Chest about 28 cm wide, 8-10 cm below the collar | Full-back wallpaper, sleeve scatter |

Charcoal or olive is the **shirt**. Do not count it as a third screen unless you print a underbase.

### Factory lock

- 2 screens. 43-55 T mesh is enough.
- Flash between cream and rust if they touch.
- Underbase cream on charcoal if the rust needs to sit bright.
- Minimum positive line 1.5 mm. No hairline knurl.
- Quotes are already outlined type in the PNG. Do not retype them.
