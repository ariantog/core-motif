# Corenation Logo Transition

**Brand:** Corenation Active — Surabaya, est. 2014  
**Use:** Brand lead + design. Pick **one** method before the next PO.  
**Related:** [`cn-logo-spec.md`](cn-logo-spec.md) · [`branding-checklist.md`](branding-checklist.md)

---

## The decision in one page

The old mark is the **closed hexagon around an N**. Regulars have seen it on belts, hems, hangtags, Shopee thumbnails, and store fixtures since the brand started.

The studio now has two families and **no locked master**: six new systems (CN abstraction + core anatomy) and five hexagon heritage variations. Do not put the whole board on product. Lock one shape, then change how it *appears*.

Your working plan is already a real method:

1. **Take the old hex off new work.** Do not reprint it.
2. **Live on the word `CORENATION`** so the name — not a drawing — holds the brand.
3. **Put the new CN only on new items**, small at first, then in the old hex slot once it feels familiar.

That is **Method A (Wordmark Bridge)**. The methods below are different ways to run the same change. Pick one. Mixing two methods on one SKU looks like a factory error.

---

## What must stay true in every method

| Lock | Rule |
|------|------|
| Name | `CORENATION` or `CORE NATION` — all caps, wide sans. Letter A may use the diagonal-cut A from the belt samples. |
| New mark | One locked direction after selection — a new system **or** one heritage hex. Cream / white / black only. |
| Old mark | Sell through existing stock. Do not reprint the original closed hex on a restock. |
| One face | Never the original hex + a new/heritage mark on the same product panel. |
| Voice | Do not say *rebrand, reborn, luxury refresh, new era*. Say the product name. The logo change is not the campaign. |
| Inventory | Dept-store apparel and unsold accs keep whatever mark they shipped with. |
| Clubs | Padel kits already cut keep the hex. New club POs follow the method you pick. |

## Method A — Wordmark Bridge *(recommended default)*

**Idea:** The drawing goes quiet. The word does the work. The new CN walks in later as a small production mark.

```
now          next drop              later drops
hex OFF  →   CORENATION only   →    CORENATION + small CN
```

**Phase 1 — word only (this PO and the next)**

- New garments and accs: no hex, no new CN.
- Belt left → right: hardware → vertical JP (if the SKU has it) → `CORENATION` → hero kanji.
- Shorts / tees: waistband or hem is tonal `CORENATION` or empty. No center badge.
- Digital you control this week: IG avatars, Shopee shop logo, hangtag reprints, hub favicon → wordmark (or wordmark + no icon).
- Store cards and bay signs: `CORENATION` only. Seasonal motif (kanji / flower / 走) stays; the hex does not.

**Phase 2 — quiet CN on new items only**

- First home for the new mark: **hangtag, care label, bag card** — surfaces customers already expect to change.
- Second home: **one new SKU family** (best: a Culture Run drop or a new belt colorway), CN at 8–12mm on hem, nape, or buckle-back. Wordmark stays the hero.
- Do not put the CN in the old hex’s old seat (belt center, waistband patch) yet.

**Phase 3 — CN takes the old seat**

- After two or three new drops have carried the small CN, it may replace the hex slot: buckle-adjacent on belts, waistband center on shorts.
- Digital avatars may switch from word-only to the new CN.

**Why it fits Corenation:** Belts already lead with `CORENATION`. Shopee titles already start with the name. You avoid a side-by-side “old vs new” argument on the same rack.

**Risk:** A season of type-only can look generic in a 1cm Shopee thumbnail. Counter: keep one hero motif (kanji / flower / run graphic) loud. The word is the brand; the motif is the product.

## Method B — Heritage refresh

**Idea:** Do not kill the hex. Stay in the closed-badge family and ship **one** of H1–H5 (Heritage, Emboss, Inline, Facet, Sideline).

```
original closed hex  →  one heritage variation, same seat
```

- Pick one variation from `assets/logo/hexagon/`. Emboss (H2) is the safest for rubber patches and tonal black-on-black.
- Keep the same cream fill, same size, same belt seat. Only outline, stance, or N construction changes.
- Do not rotate through all five hexes on consecutive POs. One lock, then stop.

**Use when:** Brand lead wants regulars to still “see Corenation” in the silhouette.

**Skip when:** You are tired of the hex and want it gone now (that is Method A).

**Risk:** Shoppers may not notice a heritage refresh. That is fine if the goal is continuity, not a new-mark story.

---

## Method C — Dual-run, then sunset

**Idea:** Old hex stays legal on **existing lines**. New CN is legal only on **new lines**. One season later, hex is retired.

| Surface | This season | Next season |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Men kanji belts / restocks already tooled | Old hex, or wordmark if the die is being remade | Wordmark + new CN |
| Women floral new colorways | Wordmark + small new CN | Same |
| Culture Run new drops | New CN only (print, not patch) | Same |
| Hijab / modest | Wordmark only | Wordmark + small CN |
| Padel kits already cut | Old hex | New PO = wordmark + CN |

**Use when:** Factory tooling and unsold GI / Muku / Cilandak stock cannot change this month.

**Risk:** One Shopee shop showing two marks. Counter: never mix them in one listing gallery. Main image follows the SKU’s own mark.

---

## Method D — Placement migration

**Idea:** The *seat* of the logo changes before the *shape* does. Eyes stop expecting a badge in the old place.

1. Remove the center hex (belt / waistband). Put `CORENATION` there.
2. New CN arrives in a **new seat**: nape, hem, buckle-back, inner waist, care label.
3. Months later, CN may move into the old center seat if you want a badge again.

**Use when:** You want zero “they changed the logo” comments. Regulars notice the word, not a new drawing.

**Pairs with:** Method A. Do not pair with Method B (heritage needs the same seat).

---

## Method E — Digital first, product last

**Idea:** Avatars, Shopee shop logo, Stories watermark, hangtags, and price cards switch first. Garments wait until current fabric is cut through.

```
week 1     IG + Shopee + hangtag = wordmark
month 2    new PO garments = wordmark
month 3+   new PO garments = wordmark + small CN
```

**Use when:** You can change files this week but the next belt die is already at the factory.

**Reverse (product first):** New CN only in-hand, digital stays hex until the first new drop is live. Use this if you do not want comments on a logo that is not on a product yet.

---

## Method F — Capsule as the vehicle

**Idea:** The new logo is not a brand announcement. It is the graphic of **one named drop**.

- Example: a Culture Run tee or a new belt colorway whose story is the mark itself (still no “rebrand” copy).
- Everything else stays wordmark-only until that drop sells and photographs clean.
- After the drop, the same CN is allowed on the next PO as a small endorsement.

**Use when:** You want a reason to show the new shape on IG without a logo-launch carousel.

**Best line for this:** Culture Run (print / dye-sub, no embroidery patch). Worst line: a restock of the current 力 belt (shoppers compare old vs new in the same listing).

---

## Method G — Split system (permanent)

**Idea:** Stop treating “logo” as one file. Split the jobs.

| Job | Mark | Where |
|-----|------|--------|
| Brand face | `CORENATION` wordmark | Shopee title, store sign, IG name, belt hero, hangtag name |
| Product / equipment mark | Locked CN | Hem, buckle-back, glove cuff, bag zipper pull, club sleeve |
| Motif | One kanji / flower / run graphic | The product story |

This is how most training brands actually work: the word is the company; the mark is the hardware stamp.

**Use when:** You want Method A to *stay* true after Phase 3 — the CN never has to become a giant chest logo.

**Do not:** Wallpaper the CN. One small stamp per piece.

---

## Method H — Tonal ghost first

**Idea:** The new CN appears as something you feel before you see.

1. Black-on-black waistband emboss, or cream-on-cream inner label.
2. Next drop: cream print at 8–12mm.
3. Later: cream or gold on the belt, same size as the old hex.

**Use when:** Men’s lifting line should not shout a new badge next to a hero kanji.

**Skip on:** Culture Run back graphics (those need a print that reads at 3m — ghost CN is too shy there; use a small front print instead).

---

## Method I — CORE / NATION split word

**Idea:** During the wordmark season, set the name as two lines:

```
CORE
NATION
```

The letters **C** and **N** become the logo before the monogram returns. Then the locked CN is the compact form of a word people already read as C + N.

**Use when:** You want the new monogram to feel obvious, not invented.

**Lock:** Same wide sans, same cream. Do not invent a third typeface. Do not add a box around CORE.

---

## What we recommend for this brand

Run **A + D + G**, in that order, once a new-system mark is locked. Do not run B (heritage) at the same time.

| Step | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| **Now** | New files and new POs: hex off. Wordmark is the only brand mark. | Do not scrap unsold hex inventory. Do not announce a rebrand. |
| **This season** | New CN only on hangtags / care labels, then on **one** new drop, in a **new** seat, 8–12mm. | Do not put CN on a restock of a hex SKU. Do not ship two CN concepts. |
| **Next season** | CN may sit in the old hex seat if the small stamp already feels normal. Or keep the split system (word = face, CN = stamp). | Do not grow the CN into an all-over monogram. |

**Do not lock a public mark yet.** Continuum is only the temporary hub icon. Circle one direction (a new system **or** one heritage hex) before Phase 2. Studio explorations stay off shop SKUs.

## Channel cheat sheet

### Shopee

- Shop logo and banners: wordmark first. CN later as a small corner mark, never both hex and CN.
- Main image: product motif readable at thumbnail size. If the garment has no badge, the kanji / flower / graphic does the work.
- Do not write “new logo” in the title. Title format does not change.

### Instagram (all three accounts)

- Avatar: wordmark monogram-as-type (C from CORENATION) **or** the locked CN — pick one per account and keep it for a full season.
- Do not run a “logo reveal” Reel as the hero. Show the product. The mark is in the frame, not the plot.
- Highlights covers: word or motif, not the retired hex.

### Stores (Central GI, Muku, Cilandak)

- Bay sign: `CORENATION MEN` / `CORENATION` + seasonal motif.
- Mannequins may still wear hex pieces until they sell. New receipts and price cards use the wordmark.
- Friday photo audit: flag any fixture that puts hex and new CN on the same face-out.

### HQ / padel

- Signed kits keep the hex. New one-pager: wordmark required; CN optional as secondary; club mark still never replaces Corenation.

## Belt and apparel map

**Old (do not draw this on new tech packs):**  
hardware → hex C → vertical JP → `CORENATION` → hero kanji

**Wordmark season:**  
hardware → vertical JP → `CORENATION` → hero kanji

**Quiet CN season:**  
hardware → small CN (or CN on buckle-back) → vertical JP → `CORENATION` → hero kanji

**Apparel:** hex off the waistband center. Wordmark tonal on the waistband **or** nothing. New CN at hem or nape only until Phase 3.

**Culture Run:** front stays quiet — printed wordmark, then a small printed CN. Back graphic stays the hero. No sewn badge.
